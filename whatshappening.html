Throughout the year, novices and experts benefit from participating in many fun and informative events and activities. It's a chance to share what you know and to give back.

Along with training classes for license exams and onsite FCC license testing, you'll find the following on our events calendar:

  • Monthly technical talks
  • Hands-on workshops on topics such as installing new services, building antennas and more
  • Local, national, and international contests
  • Field activations in fascinating places like The National World War II Museum

In the U.S. alone there are usually several ham contests each week. As a member you can participate in them at the club with our equipment. If we are participating as a club, the contest will be in our Calendar. Other contests can be found HERE.

Follow us on Facebook, X and YouTube to get the latest updates and stay connected with us!

With an annual membership of just $36, you gain full access to our range of equipment and enjoy all the perks of being part of our community.

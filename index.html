Jefferson ARC: Join the Community

Jefferson ARC fosters the craft and technology of amateur radio through mentorship and camaraderie among radio amateurs. We offer education and testing for new and existing operators and serve the community by supporting local emergency responders and providing education to the general public. Want to get involved?

Providing the Equipment you Need to Connect Globally.

Through Jefferson ARC, you'll have access to equipment that meets the needs of any experienced or novice amateur radio operator. We also provide services to any licensed amateur operator in the Greater New Orleans area, including repeaters and gateways for VHF/UHF, D-Star, Winlink, Wires-X, Fusion, and others. These services are available to our members, but also to ham operators through the Greater New Orleans area.

Our club operates a station on the Automatic Packet Reporting System (APRS), an amateur radio-based system for real-time digital communications of information of immediate value in the local area. Some of these services are provided in conjunction with the Southeast Louisiana Amateur Radio Club, Inc (SELARC).

Donations

Jefferson ARC is a 501(3)(c) and we receive a lot of equipment as donations. Some we use, but the rest we sell and put the money into our general operating funds or make planned purchases of other equipment. If you have old gear sitting around gathering dust, think about donating it to us. We also take cash :)
